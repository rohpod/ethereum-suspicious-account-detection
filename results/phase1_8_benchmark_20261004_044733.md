## 5. Table 10 Baseline Observations & Paper Anomalies

1. **Attribution to external works**: Table 10 attributes LOF to Fangfang et al. [12], CART to Saxena et al. [14], and XGBoost to Alarab et al. [16]. However, the paper reports evaluating these methods directly on the same Ethereum dataset.
2. **XGBoost baseline identity**: The Alarab et al. XGBoost baseline in Table 10 has metrics (Acc 0.975, Prec 0.970, Rec 0.980, F1 0.975, AUC 0.97) that exactly match the paper's own default XGBoost baseline in Table 8.
3. **MAE inconsistencies in paper Table 10**: For CART, paper Table 10 reports Accuracy = 0.810 and MAE = 0.22 (whereas 1 - Accuracy = 0.190). For LOF, paper Table 10 reports Accuracy = 0.949 and MAE = 0.06 (whereas 1 - Accuracy = 0.051).
4. **Unexplained 'Standard Deviation of Accuracy' column**: Table 10 lists standard deviation values (XGBoost GA: 0.109, LOF: 0.103, CART: 0.035, XGBoost default: 0.107) without explaining whether these derive from cross-validation folds, repeated seeds, or bootstrapping.
